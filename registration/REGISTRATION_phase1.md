# Registration: Ramsey lower-bound witnesses (phase 1, publish what is verified)

Public edition. The text is the original, word for word and in its original spelling, except where a line is marked
[edited]. Each edit is listed in `registration/EDITS.md` with its reason, and the SHA-256 of the full original is
recorded there.

Written 4 Nov 2026 after the fact for phase 1; the computation it covers was done 3 Nov [edited: the names of two
private working files are removed].

Purpose: a public, verified collection of the graphs behind the lower bounds in Radziszowski's dynamic survey
"Small Ramsey Numbers" (EJC DS1 rev 18, 24 Apr 2026), with a checker, and a list of bounds with no public witness.
Prior art (3 Nov scouting [edited: the path of a private working folder is removed]): McKay's page hosts many
extremal sets; the survey cites papers; no site collects a verified witness per bound. To recheck before announcing:
House of Graphs Ramsey collection, any 2026 repository (google-research ramsey_number_bounds, Lachy-Dauth).
Scope IN: two-colour R(k,l) cells of the survey tables, multicolour cells; witnesses from public files and our own
algebraic constructions (Paley, Mathon). Scope OUT: any claim that a survey entry is wrong; new search (phase 2).
Admissible sources: the survey, papers and preprints, authors' public files and repositories.
Tiers: VERIFIED (both halves checked here), PARTIAL (one half checked), CLAIMED (in the survey, no public witness found),
RECIPE (construction named, not yet built).
Redistribution: third-party files are hashed and fetched from their source, not committed, unless their licence allows.
Kill criteria: verifier disagrees with an independent oracle on small graphs; any witness verdict that two methods
dispute stays out of the VERIFIED tier.
No maintenance obligation: a dated snapshot others may maintain.
