# Edits in the public editions of the registrations

The registrations were written as working records. The public editions in this folder keep their text word for word
and in its original spelling, with the edits below and nothing else. Each edit is marked [edited] at its place in the
text. The full originals are kept by Christopher Smolen and can be compared against these SHA-256 values.

## Kinds of edit

1. **Private messages.** Where an approval or a decision quoted Christopher's own words from a private message, the
   quote is removed and the line says who approved or delegated it and when. Where the approval had a condition, the
   condition is kept in a short summary. The date and time are unchanged.
2. **Correspondence with others.** Where a line quoted or attributed something said in private correspondence by
   another person, the quote or attribution is removed. In the phase-2 "Why" line the plan is summarized in one
   clause without attribution. A planned message to another person is removed.
3. **Working files, folders and paths.** Names and paths of private working files and folders are removed. Where the
   file is in this repository, its path here replaces the working path.
4. **Working-process detail.** How work or a delegated decision was carried out is removed. What was done, who
   approved it, and every rule are unchanged.
5. **Another private project.** The name of another private project is removed.

No scope, rule, label definition, kill criterion, date or result was changed, and no count of results. Amendment 9
loses a planning target, removed with the planned message it belonged to. Amendment 11 was corrected twice
before this edition was published. The edition keeps the first correction, to its approval line, and marks the note on
the second as removed.

## What the editions are not

Phase 1 was registered after the fact (its first line says so). Phase 2 was registered before its computation. Some of
its amendments were written after the work they concern, as their text shows: Amendment 2 keeps witnesses that had
already been found, Amendment 5 applies its rule to a check that had already been run, and Amendments 10 and 11 say
they were recorded after the results. Neither registration is a
preregistration in the sense of a public, time-stamped record made before the work. These editions were first made
public with the push of the commit that adds them, released under the tag table-2026-11-22.
